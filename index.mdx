Canada sits between the two DDP routes most buyers already know. Like the United States, it has residential, commercial, warehouse and Amazon FBA destinations with different delivery rules. Like the United Kingdom, it has an import tax layer that buyers want to know is inside the price. JTLGO's Canada page frames the route around both: China warehouse intake, basic QC, export, Canadian customs, duty and GST or HST responsibility, and final-mile delivery planned as one route. This article is about the parts of a Canada DDP quote that are specific to Canada and easiest to leave vague.

## Duty and GST or HST: inside the scope, confirmed per shipment

JTLGO's answer to "does DDP to Canada include duty, GST or HST?" is careful. DDP usually places duty, GST and HST responsibility inside the quote scope, but exact inclusion, surcharges and route eligibility must be confirmed by cargo, carton data, documents and the Canadian destination.

That wording matters when you compare quotes. A Canada DDP quote should state its fee boundary in writing. JTLGO's own boundary is explicit: only items explicitly listed in the accepted itemized quotation are included, and the DDP quote explains international freight, customs clearance and the duty, GST and HST responsibility scope. If a competing quote says "DDP Canada" without stating how GST or HST is handled, you cannot tell whether the two numbers cover the same thing.

Items JTLGO lists as confirmed separately include China domestic freight, special packaging, restricted-cargo handling, FBA prep, address exceptions, extra documents, storage and consignee-specific requirements. Pickup, wooden crates, pallets, deep inspection, functional testing, third-party inspection, returns and long-term storage also sit outside the base China-side handling. The purchasing service fee is a separate line: 10% of product value with a USD 10 minimum and no minimum product value.

## The postal code and address type come first

For Canada, JTLGO's required destination data is the Canadian postal code, city, province and address type, plus Amazon Canada FBA status and any remote delivery, appointment or commercial delivery notes. The address type is not a formality. It decides which routes can be screened at all.

Manual review is triggered when the destination is Amazon Canada FBA, an appointment warehouse, a truck-delivery address, a trade show, a remote postal code or a project address. If you know any of these at the quote stage, put it in the first message. ## Amazon Canada FBA is a manual-review destination

FBA shipments to Canada are not quoted automatically. JTLGO's answer to whether it can ship to Amazon Canada FBA by DDP is that it can be manually reviewed. For that review, send the FBA warehouse code or address, carton data, SKU and FNSKU, label requirements, cargo type and FBA prep needs.

Two boundaries carry over from JTLGO's wider FBA guidance. DDP does not make Amazon the importer of record, so the importer question still needs an answer. And FBA prep — warehouse intake, basic QC photos, SKU separation, FNSKU and carton-label checks, standard repacking and recorded carton data — is its own scope, confirmed separately from the route. JTLGO's guide to [FBA-bound duty-paid shipping from China](https://jtlgo.com/amazon-fba-ddp-shipping-from-china/) covers how the prep and route decisions fit together.

## Why the price moves after warehouse intake

JTLGO is direct about why a Canada DDP price is not final before intake: supplier-estimated cartons often change. Final cost depends on measured warehouse weight, volumetric weight, packing status, restricted-cargo review and the Canadian address type. The team checks actual, volumetric and chargeable weight against the address type, the duty and tax scope, FBA label and intake needs, restricted-cargo status, packing condition and the tracking exception handoff.

The workflow it describes has six steps: submit links, photos, description, quantity, value and postal code; confirm buying, QC, consolidation, FBA prep, DDP or standard freight needs; supplier ships to the China warehouse for intake, basic QC and packing status; review carton count, weight, dimensions and repacking; screen routes by Canadian address type; confirm the fee boundary, payment, dispatch and tracking handoff.

Packing changes have limits. Adjusting packaging can support lawful protection, volume reduction or damage prevention, but it does not change the declaration, the duty and GST or HST scope, brand risk or shipping eligibility. Restricted cargo — batteries, liquids, powders, magnets, food, cosmetics, branded-sensitive goods, auto parts, machinery, oversized or fragile goods — and shipments without final carton data need manual review rather than an automatic quote.
